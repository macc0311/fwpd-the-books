## 1. Implemented Business & Scheduling Rules (Enforced Today)

### R1. Shift Count, Minimum Count & Status Classification
- **Active Shift Count (`getShiftCount()`, `index.html` `L10626-10634`):** Counts every slot across `DIV_KEYS` (`NW`, `NE`, `SW`, `SE`) where `r.name && r.pe !== null`.
- **Template Defaults (`resolveScheduleTemplate()`, `index.html` `L10325-10343`):**
  - Operations `A`-Shift: Minimum `32` (`A_11_15` starts at 32; `A_11_17` starts at 36)
  - Operations `B`-Shift: Minimum `40` (`B_11_16` starts at 42)
  - Operations `C`-Shift: Minimum `34` (`C_11_15` starts at 36)
  - Detective Bureau Flex/Day: Minimum `30` (`DB_FLEX` starts at 48)
  - Detective Bureau C-Shift: Minimum `2` (`DB_C` starts at 5)
- **Status Banner States (`renderAll()`, `index.html` `L11849-11861`):**
  - `count === min`: **MINIMUM COUNT** (amber banner; spot leave blocked).
  - `count < min`: **BELOW MINIMUM (`count`/`min`) • + HIRE BACK ▾** (red pulsing banner; auto-triggers Hire-Back broadcast, opens `#hbZeroTouchControlBar` countdown posting, and pauses floating).
  - `count > min`: **ABOVE MINIMUM (+`count - min` OPEN)** (emerald banner; spot leave allowed).

### R2. Below-Minimum Float Pause Guard (Commit `6123c40`)
- **Rule (`recalculateFloats()`, `index.html` `L10679`):** Floating executes **only when `currentCount >= app.shiftMin`**.
- **Operational Effect:** When a shift falls below minimum (`count < min`), all remaining on-duty officers stay in their normal home car assignments, and the vacated car stays marked `OPEN HB` (`vacatedDayOffEntry`) so the awarded Hire-Back officer fills that vacated role directly without displacing coworkers. Adjusting `app.shiftMin` (`changeMinimum`, `setMinForVacationDemo`) also re-runs `recalculateFloats()` so restoring `count >= shiftMin` immediately resumes required district floats.
- **UI Synchronization:** `#floatQueueMiniList` (`index.html` `L12669-12673`) and `#mobileAppBody` (`L12619-12620`) explicitly display that floating is paused while `count < min`.

### R3. Inverse PE# Seniority Floating & Protection Rules (`recalculateFloats()`, `index.html` `L10680-10744`)
When `count >= min`, for each division (`NW`, `NE`, `SW`, `SE`) and each district in `DIV_DISTRICTS[div]` (`A–C`, `E–G`, `I–L`, `D/M–O`) where `distCounts[dist] === 0`:
1. **Target Slot Preference (`L10686-10689`):** Vacated day-off unit in that district → open non-bid car in that district → any open car in that district.
2. **Protected Cars Never Float (`L10695-10699`, `L12644-12646`):**
   - **Standing Union Bid Car (`r.bidCar === true`):** Locked against floating (marked bold/underlined with emerald bar via `.unit-bid-car`).
   - **Sole District Car (`distCounts[r.district] <= 1`):** Last remaining car covering a district cannot be floated away.
3. **Donor Selection Order (`L10704-10711`):**
   - **`citywide` scope (default):** Highest `pe` first (`b.row.pe - a.row.pe` — lowest seniority floats first).
   - **`quadrant` scope:** Same-division eligible officers (`a.div === div`) prioritized before out-of-division officers, then highest `pe` first.
4. **Deterministic Replay (`L10656-10674`):** `recalculateFloats()` resets all prior dynamic floats and recomputes from baseline on every roster change, leaving `isHireBack` and `isReturnedOffTime` placements intact.

### R4. Leave Request Gate & Minimum-Count Bypass Rules (`LEAVE_CONFIG_MAP`, `index.html` `L11349-11398`)
- **Minimum-Gated Leave (`requiresAboveMin: true`):**
  - `DAY_OFF_VAC` (Vacation — Art. 43 §4)
  - `DAY_OFF_PERS` (Personal Day — Art. 44 §1)
  - `DAY_OFF_FLSA` (FLSA / Comp Time — Art. 39 §2)
  - Held/blocked by `handleOfficerStatusSelect()` (`L11649-11659`) when `currentCount <= minCount` (demo override button calls `forceApprovePendingDayOff()` at `L11664-11671`, decrementing `app.shiftMin`).
- **Minimum-Bypass Leave (`requiresAboveMin: false`):**
  - `SICK` (Sick Leave Call-In — Art. 50 & Art. 35 §3)
  - `BEREAVEMENT` (Bereavement Leave — Art. 45)
  - `UTB` (Union Time Bank — Art. 16 §1)
  - Approved even at or below minimum count; if `countAfter < shiftMin`, auto-triggers Hire-Back broadcast (`L11716-11723`).
- **Partial Duty Codes (`L11618-11644`):** `CODE_88` (`88 Off-Duty`) and `CODE_87` (`87 On-Duty`) prompt for a 4-digit time and stamp `row.assign` inline without removing the officer from `getShiftCount()`.

### R5. Unified Scheduled Off-Time & Return-to-Work Rules
- **Unified 4-Column Off-Time Sheet (`renderPaperScheduledOffTimeRows()`, `index.html` `L12230-12291`):**
  - Columns: `dayOff` (`DAY OFF (ROTATION)`), `vacation` (`PREF. VACATION / VACATION / PERSONAL / NFLSA`), `sickAndLeave` (`SICK / BEREAVEMENT / LIGHT DUTY / MILITARY`), `trainingAndSpecial` (`TRAINING / ADMIN / EXCH / UNION / SPECIAL`).
  - Dynamic marked-off officers (`app.dayOffRoster`) prepend above static `app.offTimeSheet` rows inside their inferred category column (`inferOffTimeCategory()`).
- **Return-to-Work & Reassignment:**
  - Dynamic off-time officers can be restored directly to their `homeUnit` via `restoreOfficerFromDayOff()` (`L11752-11783`), clearing any temporary `isHireBack` / `isReturnedOffTime` occupant on that `homeUnit`.
  - Static off-time officers can cancel off-time via `returnStaticOfficerToWork()` (`L11449-11489`, either regular `RTW` or `1.5x OT` Hire-Back) or move between columns via `moveStaticOffTimeOfficer()` (`L11491-11502`).
  - Temporary duty (`RTW` or `HIREBACK`) rows can be reverted via `cancelTemporaryDutyRow()` (`L11504-11536`), which re-checks shortages and re-opens Hire-Back postings when reverting below minimum.
- **Open Slot Placement Priority (`findTargetOpenSlotForReturnOrHireBack()`, `index.html` `L11400-11446`):**
  1. Explicit `(preferredDiv, preferredUnit)` if empty; 2. Officer's known home unit in `MASTER_OPERATIONS_DIRECTORY`; 3. Vacated day-off unit in an uncovered district (`distCounts[r.district] === 0`); 4. Any vacated day-off unit; 5. Open non-header car in uncovered district; 6. Slot whose occupant floated out; 7. First open non-bid car; 8. First open car.

### R6. Searchable Cross-Shift Database & Manual Hire-Back (`index.html` `L10952-11605`)
- **Opt-In Registry:** `app.hireBackOptInPes` (seeded with 11 `DEFAULT_HIREBACK_OPT_IN_PES`). Opting out removes pending bids for that PE.
- **Dual Award Ranking (`getSortedHireBackBids()`, `L11027-11041`):** `SENIORITY_24HR` (`≥24h` lowest PE# wins) vs `FCFS_UNDER_24HR` (`<24h` earliest `bidOrder` wins).
- **Searchable Officer Database Popover (`openHireBackPopover()`, `L11105-11334`, commits `66f685c` & `4dcbd71`):**
  - Filter tabs strictly ordered **`A` | `B` | `C` | `All`**; excludes `role === 'COMMAND'` and active on-duty PEs; deduplicates by PE# ascending; no Quick Award section.
- **Manual / Database Award Sync (`awardCrossShiftHireBack()` wrapped by `js/hireback-engine.js` `L2236-2264`):** Places winner (`isHireBack = true`), recalculates floats, closes the matching open `OFFICER` countdown posting (preventing duplicate auto-awards), records notification receipt, updates Mobile App banner (`app.hireBackLastWinnerNotification`), and preserves the award toast.

### R7. Zero-Touch Hire-Back Countdown, Eligibility & Partial Blocks (`js/hireback-engine.js`)
- **Notice Modes & Timers (`L38-117`, `L413-502`, `L1307-1368`):**
  - `ADVANCE_24PLUS` (`≥24h`): Opens a **`12s`** bid collection window (`BID_WINDOW_SECONDS = 12`); awards automatically at `0s` with **Uniform (`OPS`) priority** (lowest PE# first) before DB/Exempt tiers (`sortBidsForPosting`, `L216-246`), or strict DB-only restriction on Detective Bureau shifts (`L205-210`).
  - `SHORT_UNDER24` (`<24h`): Starts with **`0` bids** (`ensureAutomatedHireBackBroadcast` suppresses fake auto-seeds); awards immediately upon first claim (`Instant Manual Claim`) department-wide including Detectives & Exempt/Narcotics on `OPS` (`FCFS`).
  - `SHIFT_STARTED` (`FOPLC Art. 24 §4D`): Holds new/open postings as `HELD_DUTY_CHIEF` until `toggleDutyChiefAuth()` unlocks them.
- **Contract Bidder Exclusion Guards (`evaluateBidderEligibility()`, `L158-214` & `L2290-2336`):**
  - **Self-Mark-Off Exclusion (`PBA Art. 35 §4`):** Officers in `app.dayOffRoster` or `app.hbEngine.commandOffRoster` are blocked from bidding or instant-claiming their own shift across all UI paths (Mobile App, Searchable Database, Bid Queue sync).
  - **Sick / Light Duty Exclusion (`FOPLC Art. 26`):** Blocked while marked sick/light-duty.
  - **Already On-Duty / Role / Bureau Match:** Prevents double-booking and enforces `SUPERVISOR` (`LT`/`SGT`, or `LT`-only for partial-LT) vs bargaining-unit `OFFICER`/`DETECTIVE`/`EXEMPT`.
- **Unfilled Split & C-Shift AM Training Exceptions (`L805-836`, `L1263-1302`):**
  - If a full-shift `OFFICER` bid window expires with `0` bids, `splitUnfilledPostingIntoPartialBlocks()` automatically replaces it with `FIRST_4H` and `LAST_4H` FCFS postings (`PBA Art. 35 §2`).
  - `triggerCShiftTrainingLeave()` credits the last `4 hrs` off (`IN-SVC TRAIN (LAST 4H)`, `PBA Art. 34 §7`) and posts **only** a `LAST_4H` FCFS Hire-Back (`PBA Art. 35 §2`).
- **Un-Awarded Cancellation Notifications (`cancelUnAwardedPostings()`, `L841-980`, commit `bf7d6f0`):** Whenever a returning officer (`restoreOfficerFromDayOff`, `returnStaticOfficerToWork`, `returnOfficerToCancelHireBack`) or lowered minimum restores `count >= shiftMin` before award, un-awarded `HB-*` postings cancel automatically and dispatch cancellation receipts to all `app.hireBackOptInPes` & pending bidders across top strip, Hire-Back card, and Mobile App.

### R8. FOPLC Supervisor Minimums, Auto Acting COD & Command Hire-Backs (`js/hireback-engine.js` `L251-357`, `L1103-1258`)
- **Command Minimum Audit (`auditSupervisorMinimums()`, `L251-357`):** On `OPS` shifts, enforces **`1 LT` (COD) + `4 Uniform Sergeants` (`≥1` per quadrant `NW/NE/SW/SE`) = `5` total supervisors** (`FOPLC Art. 24 §2A–B`).
- **Auto Acting COD (`FOPLC Art. 24 §2A & Art. 25`):** When `0 LT` are working and `workingSgts.length > 0`, automatically elevates the **most senior Sergeant (lowest PE#)** to `ACTING COD` (`assign = "ACTING COD"`, `.row-acting-cod`). If `≥5` Sergeants remain and all 4 quadrants have `≥1` Sgt, no supervisor overtime Hire-Back is posted; otherwise (`<5` Sgts or any quadrant at `0`), opens a `SUPERVISOR` Hire-Back posting (`QUADRANT_SGT` or `COD_AND_QUADRANT_SGT`).
- **Partial LT & Seniority Standby List:** Marking an LT partial-shift (`88`) with `<5` Sergeants posts a `FIRST_4H` `LT`-only Hire-Back (`FOPLC Art. 24 §3`); unawarded supervisor bidders populate `app.hbEngine.supervisorStandbyList` for sequential auto-fill (`FOPLC Art. 24 §4B`).

### R9. Auto-Persistence, 1-Page Print & Export Rules (`js/persistence-print-export.js`)
- **Auto-Save/Restore:** Debounce-free save to `localStorage` (`fwpd_the_books_state_v3_0_unified`) on every render/preference toggle; `resetSchedule()` clears storage back to defaults.
- **1-Page US Letter Landscape Roll-Call Print (`css/print-rollcall.css`):** `triggerRollCallPrint()` enters `body.rollcall-print-preview`, injects `#printRollCallFooter` (A–O district coverage + active floats + legend + COD signature line), suppresses interactive UI & `.row-logged-in` tint, and provides `downloadPrintableRollCallHtml()` as an iframe-safe fallback.
- **Portable Exports:** Downloads timestamped `.json` full-state backup (`fwpd-the-books-<shift>-<date>.json`), roster/day-off CSV (`fwpd-roster-<shift>-<date>.csv`), and contract audit CSV (`fwpd-contract-audit-log-<date>.csv`), with full `.json` import restore.

#### 2.2.2 Seniority, Bidding, Probation & Temporary Assignments
- **PBA Seniority & Attrition (`Art. 21 §§1–3`, `Art. 9 §§1–3`):** Continuous FWPD appointment date; tiebreak alphabetical by last name at appointment. Broken only by quit (>6 mo), 12-mo layoff, or discharge. Layoffs inverse seniority w/ ≥4 wks union notice; recalls direct seniority.

Two-phase award: **Primary** (Div/Shift/Bureau) + **Secondary** (section or standing district bid car `A–C, E–G, I–L, M–O` + new annexed districts). Specialized units (K-9, SRO, CS, Hit-Skip) bid unit-first. Crime Suppression & Crimes Against Persons exempt (excl. Dec 31, 2004 grandfathered; open Crime Suppression spots offered to Property Crimes detectives first, else bid to A-shift DB). Intranet posting `7 cal days`; max `3` simultaneous bids / **`3 awarded bids/yr`**; suspended `>30 days` barred. Trial rescission **`10 cal days`** (`15 cal days` post-training + `15-day` extension). Displaced officers bump junior in div/section (else B-shift Uniform) with 6-mo re-establishment return rights; K-9 & SRO never inverse force-filled.
- **FOPLC Seniority & Bidding (`Art. 9 §§1,3,4`):** Most recent Merit Commission Sgt/Lt appointment date; tiebreak Merit Promotion List rank. Post vacancies within `15 cal days` (Ops Sgt: Div/Sec/Shift/Rank; DB: Div/Shift/Rank after optional same-div lateral by seniority; Lt: Div/Shift/Rank); start ≤`21 days`; **`10 cal days`** rescission (delayed to first return workday if on FMLA); displaced/demoted/disciplined Sgts temp-assigned by Asst Chief to Uniform A/B/C until bid (cannot inverse disciplined Sgt back to same job). **Exempt supervisors:** max `25% + 1`, `≥3 mo` in rank (excl. IA/Academy/CS/Spillman), 5-day notice, `7-day` rescission. On-call extra-duty ties resolved by seniority (excl. PIO).
- **Temporary & Intra-Shift Assignments:**
  - **PBA (`Art. 28`):** Max **`8`** dept-wide, **`90 cal days/yr`** (`160 days` for 1 Academy instructor & up to 2 SROs). Intra-shift motor/guard/desk/supply/bike/foot assignments use **inverse seniority**, moving **unbid officers first**.
  - **FOPLC (`Art. 10 §4`):** Max **`4`** supervisors dept-wide, max **`1`** per Shift/Div, **`90 cal days / 12 mo`** (`160` Academy / `180` SRO), `≥3 mo` in rank (excl. IA/Academy/CS/Spillman).
