# PBA Rules
*(Source: 2026 Fort Wayne Patrolmen's Benevolent Association Contract — Rank below Sergeant)*

## 1. Shift Scheduling & Hours of Work

- **Definition of a Day / Shift ([Article 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L9-L25)):**
  - A working "Day" consists of one working shift of 8, 8.5, or 10 hours depending on assignment.
  - A "Shift" is a scheduled period of work or duty.
- **Uniform Operations Standard Schedule ([Article 34, Section 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L400)):**
  - **Rotation:** 4 days on, 2 days off (4-2 schedule) at 8.5-hour shifts.
  - **Standing Shifts:**
    - **A Shift:** 0600 – 1430
    - **B Shift:** 1400 – 2230
    - **C Shift:** 2200 – 0630
- **Investigative Division Schedule ([Article 34, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L402) & [Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L404-L405)):**
  - **A Shift (Days):** 5 days on, 2 days off (5-2 schedule), Monday through Friday, at 8.0-hour shifts from 0800 – 1600 (or 0700 – 1500 at the Investigative Commander's discretion).
  - **B Shift (Evenings):** 4 days on, 2 days off (4-2 schedule), at 8.5-hour shifts from 1500 – 2330.
  - **C Shift (Midnight):** 4 days on, 2 days off (4-2 schedule), at 8.5-hour shifts from 2300 – 0730.
  - **Weekend Duty Coverage ([Article 34, Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L404-L405); [Article 22, Section 1(G)](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L263)):** Monday–Friday (8-hour) employees required to cover Saturday and Sunday take 1 day off in the work week prior to the weekend and 1 day off in the work week following the weekend at the employee's discretion, subject to minimum counts. Juvenile Aid detectives are included in Investigative Division A-shift minimum counts for weekend duties and holiday assignments only.
- **Specialized Unit Schedules:**
  - **School Resource Officers (SRO) ([Article 24, Sections 4, 7–10](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L306-L318)):** Assigned to Community Relations Division on A Shift; 5 days on, 2 days off (Monday–Friday), 8.0-hour shifts from 0700 – 1500. Within 7 days of the last day of school, all SROs (except up to 2 concentrating on FWCS summer needs) are assigned to Operations Division for Uniform Patrol duties in a district car during their normal work hours, returning to schools the earlier of "Transition Day" or 7 days before the first day of school.
  - **Hit-Skip Officers ([Article 25, Sections 2–3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L324-L326)):** Minimum of 3 Hit-Skip officers working 5 days on, 2 days off (Monday–Friday), at 8.0 hours per shift.
  - **Crime Scene Officers/Technicians ([Article 26, Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L336)):** 5 days on, 2 days off.
    - **A Shift:** 0800 – 1600
    - **B Shift:** 1600 – 2400
    - **Mid-Shift:** 1800 – 0200
  - **K-9 Unit ([Article 23, Sections 4–6](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L288-L292)):** Assigned to uniform patrol sections of the four Divisions on A, B, and C shifts (or exempt in Vice/Narcotics). At the end of each 7-day cycle, K-9 officers receive 4 additional hours at their regular hourly rate for "K-9 Maintenance and Care."
- **Exempt & Emergency Schedules ([Article 29](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L370); [Article 34, Sections 4–5](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L407-L409)):**
  - Up to 74 bargaining unit employees may be assigned to exempt positions at the Chief's discretion (none in Internal Affairs), with work hours set at the Chief's discretion.
  - The Chief has the right to temporarily change normal working hours due to emergency situations.
- **Flexing Shift Hours ([Article 34, Section 6](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L411-L414)):**
  - Employees may flex shift hours by no more than 2 hours at the start or end of their shift (or >2 hours voluntarily with Employer approval) only after a specific plan is developed and approved by an on-shift Supervisor, Shift Commander, Deputy Chief, or Chief of Police.
  - Flexing is solely at the employee's discretion; no shift time may be flexed without the employee's specific approval.
  - Make-work assignments or assignments created solely to flex time for school, childcare, sports, or fitness are prohibited, and no flex assignment is permitted if it impacts shift minimum counts.
- **In-Service Training Following C Shift ([Article 34, Section 7](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L416)):**
  - If assigned to in-service training during A-shift hours immediately following a scheduled C shift, the employee is marked off-duty for the last 4 hours of the prior C shift and may mark off the first half of that shift (4.5 hours for Operations / 4 hours for Detective Bureau) using available banked time.
- **Temporary Assignments & Intra-Shift Reassignments ([Article 28](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L363-L367)):**
  - Maximum of 8 temporary assignments in use at any one time unless agreed in writing.
  - Maximum of 90 calendar days on temporary assignment per employee per calendar year (no limit on number of assignments within 90 days), except 1 employee may serve up to 160 consecutive days instructing at the Police Academy and up to 2 employees may serve up to 160 days as SROs while school is in session (both count toward the 8-assignment cap).
  - Intra-shift movements for motor, guard duty, desk duty, supply room duty, bicycle patrol, or foot patrol are not temporary assignments; they are assigned by **inverse seniority** unless a senior officer requests the duty. Unbid/unassigned officers placed on a shift are always moved first regardless of seniority.
- **Exchange of Work Time ([Article 38, Sections 1–4](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L481-L488); [Article 16, Section 6](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L189)):**
  - Officers may exchange work time with officers in the same like work (uniform with uniform, investigative with investigative) up to **160 hours** per year for a 5-2 rotation and **170 hours** per year for a 4-2 rotation (20 days of exchange time). Holidays are excluded from these limits.
  - Both dates of an exchange must fall within **30 days** of each other.
  - Exchange forms must be signed by both employees (or submitted electronically) and submitted to the Commander of the Day (COD) at least **24 hours prior** to the exchanged shift for Shift Commander approval. Once approved, the form is binding.
  - **Emergency Exception:** Twice per year, an employee may submit an Exchange of Work Time form inside the 24-hour window for emergency situations.
  - **Military Drill Exchanges ([Article 47, Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L596)):** Employees may exchange scheduled military drill days with regular days off and pay back the exchanged day(s) by working a scheduled day off in the rotation immediately prior to and/or following the drill.

## 2. Seniority & Tie-Breakers

- **Seniority Definition & Tie-Breaker ([Article 21, Section 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L224); [Article 22, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L275)):**
  - Seniority is determined by the most recent date of continuous appointment with the Fort Wayne Police Department.
  - **Tie-Breaker:** If two or more employees have the exact same employment/appointment date, seniority ranking is determined by an **alphabetized listing of the employees by last name at the time of appointment**.
- **Termination of Seniority ([Article 21, Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L233-L236)):**
  - Seniority is broken only if an employee: (A) quits and is not reappointed within 6 months, (B) is laid off for 12 consecutive months, or (C) is discharged for just cause.
- **Probationary Bidding Eligibility ([Article 21, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L226-L227); [Article 22, Section 1(H)–(I)](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L264-L265)):**
  - Probation is 12 months (6 months supervised, 6 months unsupervised). Probationary employees may exercise bid rights starting in the final month of supervised probation.
  - Approximately 1 month before probationary employees finish field training, department-wide bids up to the number of employees coming off probation are opened until no employee is left placed on a shift. Jobs held by probationary employees open to bid by all employees upon completion of field training.
- **Job Bidding & Seniority Assignment Rules ([Article 22, Sections 1–3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L246-L278); [Article 9, Section 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L118-L119)):**
  - Non-exempt vacancies are awarded by seniority in a two-phased format:
    1. **Primary Bid ([Article 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L20); [Article 22, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L273)):** Simultaneous bid for Division, Shift, and/or Bureau (or specialized section like K-9, SRO, Hit-Skip).
    2. **Secondary Bid ([Article 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L21); [Article 22, Section 1(B)–(D)](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L248-L259)):** Bid for a specific section or standing union bid patrol car within the Division (at least 1 bid car per district: N/W Districts A, B, C; N/E Districts E, F, G; S/W Districts I, J, K, L; S/E Districts M, N, O; plus 1 per shift in any newly annexed district).
  - **Unit-First Bidding ([Article 22, Section 1(E)](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L260); [Article 23, Section 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L282); [Article 24, Section 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L300); [Article 26, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L334)):** Openings in specialized units (K-9, SRO, Crime Scene, Hit-Skip, etc.) are bid within the respective unit before being bid department-wide.
  - **Investigative Division Exceptions ([Article 22, Section 1(F)](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L261-L262)):** Section work on all shifts is bid by seniority except Crime Suppression and Crimes Against Persons (which are exempt, save for officers grandfathered as of Dec 31, 2004). Open Crime Suppression detective spots may be filled by selecting a current Property Crimes detective; if none is selected, it is bid by seniority to the A-shift Detective Bureau.
  - **Bid Posting & Limits ([Article 22, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L270-L274)):** Department-wide bids remain open on the intranet for 7 consecutive calendar days. Officers may bid on at most 3 jobs simultaneously and are limited to **3 awarded bids per calendar year** (unsuccessful bids do not count). Officers under suspension >30 days cannot bid.
  - **Rescission & Trial Period ([Article 22, Section 1(H) & Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L264-L278)):** An officer may withdraw a bid any time before the posted start date, and has **10 calendar days** from the physical start date to rescind and return to their previous primary bid, district, and rotation (or **15 calendar days** after completing required training and final evaluation for positions requiring training, with a 15-day extension available to either party). Rescinded bids go to the next senior bidder.
  - **Unfilled Openings & Job Elimination / Bumping ([Article 9, Section 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L118-L119); [Article 22, Section 1(L)](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L268)):** After one department-wide bid for a new/vacant position, the most junior employee may be required (inversed) to fill the open job (K-9 and SRO positions can never be force-filled by inverse seniority per [Article 23, Section 4](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L288) and [Article 24, Section 4](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L306)). If a position is eliminated, the displaced officer may exercise seniority on available unfilled positions or bump the next junior employee in the division or section (if none, placed in B-Shift Uniform). If re-established within 6 months, the displaced officer gets first opportunity to return.
  - **Layoff & Recall ([Article 9, Sections 2–3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L121-L124)):** Layoffs occur in inverse order of seniority (last hired, first laid off) with at least 4 weeks' notice to the Union; recalls occur in direct order of seniority (last laid off, first recalled).

## 3. Overtime Rotation, Hire Backs & Special Events

- **Standard Overtime & FLSA Work Periods ([Article 39, Sections 1–3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L492-L501)):**
  - Work beyond a regularly scheduled shift is compensated hour-for-hour (calculated to the next quarter hour) unless it qualifies for FLSA overtime (1.5x), holiday pay, court time, or special event pay.
  - **7-Day Section 207(k) Work Periods (starting Sunday A-Shift):**
    - **4-2 Schedule (8.5-hr shifts):** 1.5x FLSA overtime after **42.5 hours** worked in a 7-day period (to the next quarter hour).
    - **4-3 Schedule (10-hr shifts):** 1.5x FLSA overtime after **40 hours** worked in a 7-day period.
    - **5-2 Schedule (8-hr shifts):** 1.5x FLSA overtime after **40 hours** worked in a 7-day period (to the next quarter hour).
  - **FLSA "Hours Worked" Base ([Article 39, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L498)):** Includes hours actually worked, FLSA and Non-FLSA hours used, PBA leave time (other than conventions/seminars), and paid holidays. Does **not** include vacation days, sick days, bereavement leave, or personal days.
  - **Off-Duty Vehicle Repair ([Article 39, Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L501)):** C-shift officers (or others directed by command) required to bring a police vehicle in for scheduled repair outside normal working hours when the shop is closed during their shift are compensated under Article 39.
- **Hire-Back Procedures (Staffing Below Minimum Count) ([Article 35, Sections 1–5](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L420-L435)):**
  - **Trigger & Rate ([Article 35, Sections 2, 4, 5](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L422-L435)):** Whenever shift count falls below posted minimum count, the Employer must hire back to bring the shift to minimum count. Paid at **1.5x** normal rate of pay. An officer cannot be hired back to a shift from which they marked off.
  - **Uniform Division Hire-Back Rotation ([Article 35, Section 4](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L428-L432)):**
    1. **≥ 24 Hours' Notice:** Posted as a department-wide bid and filled by **seniority**, with priority given to officers bid or assigned to a Uniform Division or Shift.
    2. **Unfilled Within 24 Hours of Shift Start:** Notification is sent out and filled on a **first-come, first-served** basis department-wide (including Detectives and exempt uniform officers such as Gang and Narcotics).
    3. **< 24 Hours' Notice:** Announced via Uniform Division and Investigative police radio channels and in-car messaging system, filled first-come, first-served.
  - **Detective Bureau Hire-Back Rotation ([Article 35, Section 4](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L428-L433)):**
    1. **≥ 24 Hours' Notice:** Posted bid to the Detective Bureau (including weekend duty shortages), filled by **seniority**.
    2. **< 24 Hours' Notice:** Notification sent to the Detective Bureau and filled on a **first-come, first-served** basis.
  - **Partial Shift Hire-Backs ([Article 35, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L423-L424)):**
    - If a full-shift hire-back is not filled, partial-shift hire-backs (minimum 4 hours: first 4 hours, last 4 hours, or both at COD's discretion) are offered on a **first-come, first-served** basis.
    - **C-Shift Training Exception:** When C-shift officers are given administrative time off for morning training on the same day, only a 4-hour hire-back for the last 4 hours of C shift is offered (skipping the full-shift offer).
- **Special Events Staffing & Overtime Rotation ([Article 27](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L344-L361); [Article 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L13)):**
  - **Definition & Notice:** Regular occurrence activities requiring personnel redeployment that cannot be staffed from on-duty personnel without dropping below minimum count (excludes guard duty, directed patrols, warming centers, City Council meetings). The Chief must notify the Union of anticipated Special Events by March 1 (or within 15 days of rescheduling notice after Feb 18), bid events at least **45 days in advance**, and post awarded bids and inversed officers no later than **15 days prior** to the event.
  - **Order of Staffing ([Article 27](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L347-L359)):**
    1. **Seniority Bidding:** Awarded by seniority. (If Bicycle-certified officers are used, under 50% of the event's staffing may be bid specifically for Bike-certified officers—e.g., max 4 of 10).
    2. **Celebrity/Dignitary Visits:** Staffed by EST and PSRT first; if additional officers are needed with <45 days' notice, standard overtime bidding is used.
    3. **Department-Wide Inverse Seniority:** If bidding is insufficient, department-wide union inverse seniority is used.
       - **Exemptions from Special Event Inversing:** Undercover officers ([Article 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L13); [Article 27](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L351)), officers working a full shift the day of the event, officers on a one-time Preferential Bid vacation ([Article 27](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L350)), and officers already inversed to work a holiday on that same day even if they exchanged the holiday ([Article 27](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L354)).
  - **Special Event Compensation & Cancellation ([Article 27](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L352-L361)):**
    - Paid at **double time (2.0x)**, calculated by dividing `(base salary + shift differential + longevity pay + specialty duty bonuses)` by `2080`.
    - On a holiday, Special Event work receives holiday premium pay, plus a holiday "day back" if working 4 or more hours.
    - If cancelled with **< 24 hours' notice**, scheduled officers receive **2 hours' pay** at the Special Event rate. If cancelled after reporting (or relieved without fault), the officer contacts the COD for reassignment or is paid for the balance of the event.

## 4. Minimum Staffing

- **Establishment & Posting of Minimums ([Article 37, Sections 1–3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L469-L473); [Article 53, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L650)):**
  - The Employer must maintain minimum staffing levels for work shifts and post them, explaining the methodology in writing to the Union.
  - Shift and quadrant minimum staffing levels are provided to the Union on **May 1** and **October 1** each year (October 1 minimums may include fluctuating winter minimums).
  - **Day After Thanksgiving ([Article 37, Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L473)):** Minimum count for 5-2 and 4-3 employees is reduced to **one-half (1/2)** the normal minimum count for all sections involved.
  - **During Special Events ([Article 27](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L347-L357)):** Uniform minimum counts may not be increased by more than 2 during a Special Event, and time off must be granted above minimum counts.
- **Exclusions from Daily Minimum Count:**
  - **K-9 Officers ([Article 23, Section 8](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L296)):** Excluded from daily shift minimum counts and holiday minimum counts (unless working a holiday exchange for an inversed officer, in which case they count toward minimum and patrol a designated district).
  - **School Resource Officers (SRO) ([Article 24, Section 6](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L310)):** Excluded from daily shift minimum counts and holiday minimum counts (unless working a holiday exchange for an inversed officer, in which case they count toward minimum and patrol a designated district).
  - **Military Leave > 15 Days ([Article 37, Section 4](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L475); [Article 47, Section 5](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L600)):** Officers serving in the National Guard or U.S. Reserve for over 15 consecutive days do not count toward or negatively impact shift minimum counts in their Division, Section, or Unit.
  - **Union Time Bank (UTB) Exceptions ([Article 16, Sections 1, 4](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L177-L185)):** The PBA President works 50% in their bid assignment and 50% on Union business (tracked via Flex Schedule Report for minimum counts). General UTB usage is subject to minimum counts, **except**:
    - The PBA President, Vice President, Treasurer, and Secretary may use UTB hours even if minimum counts are impacted.
    - PBA Board members may use UTB hours regardless of minimum counts if requested at least **15 days in advance** (requests within 15 days are subject to minimum counts).
  - **Extended Bereavement Leave ([Article 45](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L580-L581)):** Up to 5 vacation/personal days for extended family funerals/bereavement, plus up to 4 additional days (or days to complete a work rotation) using unused personal/vacation/FLSA/Non-FLSA time, must be granted **not subject to minimum counts**.
- **District Floating Priority ([Article 37, Section 5](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L477)):**
  - When officers are temporarily "floated" to another district due to manpower shortages:
    1. Officers **not bid to the shift** must float first, regardless of seniority.
    2. Officers **bid to the shift** are floated by inverse seniority.
    3. Officers occupying a **Union bid car** shall **not** be required to float.

## 5. Callback / Call-Out & Court Pay

- **Call-Out Definition ([Article 40, Section 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L505)):**
  - An immediate response to an emergency situation, warrant service, protective detail, or any other situation deemed necessary by the Chief or designee with **48 hours' notice or less**.
- **Call-Out Bonus Pay ([Article 40, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L507); [Article 27](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L355)):**
  - **Begins AND Ends Outside Regular Shift:** Bonus of **2 paid hours** (in addition to actual hours worked).
  - **Either Begins OR Ends Outside Regular Shift:** Bonus of **1 paid hour** (in addition to actual hours worked).
  - **Separation Rule:** There must be at least 1 hour separating call-outs; additional call-outs occurring within the 1-hour separation receive a minimum of **1 paid hour**.
  - **Holiday Call-Out ([Article 40, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L507); [Article 36, Section 8](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L465)):** Officers paged out or requested to come in on a holiday receive Holiday Pay (double time). If called out for **4 or more hours** on a holiday, the officer also receives the **holiday back**.
  - **Special Event Call-Out ([Article 27](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L355)):** Receives Special Event double-time pay for actual hours worked **plus** the Article 40 call-out bonus.
- **On-Call Rotation & Standby Bonuses ([Article 26, Section 6](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L342); [Article 40, Sections 3–4](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L509-L518)):**
  - **Homicide & Air Support ([Article 40, Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L509)):**
    - 8-hour shift employees receive an 8-hour call-out bonus day split between primary week (4 hours) and back-up week (4 hours).
    - 8.5-hour (4-2) shift employees receive an 8.5-hour call-out bonus day split between primary week (4.25 hours) and back-up week (4.25 hours).
  - **Crime Scene Call-Out Team ([Article 26, Section 6](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L342); [Article 40, Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L509)):** Consists of 2 team members, trainee(s), and 1 supervisor on a 2-week rotation; receives 4 hours for the first/primary week and 4 hours for the second/back-up week (8 hours total).
  - **Fatal Crash Investigation Team ([Article 40, Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L510)):** Receives a 4-hour call-out bonus for the primary week (4.25 hours for 8.5-hour 4-2 shift employees).
  - **Annual Specialty Team On-Call Time Bank ([Article 40, Section 4](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L512-L518)):** Credited at the start of each year (at 8 or 8.5 hours per day depending on shift):
    - **EST:** 4 days
    - **CRT:** 4 days
    - **EOD:** 2 days
    - **PIO:** 2 days
    - **PSRT:** 1 day per call-out lasting >4 hours (max 1 day back per 24 hours of a call-out).
    - *Attendance & Forfeiture Rule:* Requires ≥60% call-out attendance rate for the year (excluding prolonged illness/injury), or the following year's allotment is forfeited. All hours earned in a calendar year must be used prior to the subsequent year's Non-FLSA paydown or they are forfeited.
- **Off-Duty Internal Affairs / OPS Interviews ([Article 7, Section 6](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L82)):**
  - Officers ordered into IA, OPS, or the Department while off-duty for questioning are compensated for the time spent in the interview **plus 1 hour of travel time**.
- **Off-Duty Court & Pre-Trial Appearance Pay ([Article 41, Sections 1–2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L522-L524)):**
  - **Within Allen County:** Paid at **double time (2.0x)** with a **1-hour minimum** per appearance (not per subpoena), rounded up to the nearest quarter hour beyond the first hour.
  - **Outside Allen County:** Same 2.0x compensation (1-hour minimum) for the appearance, **plus** travel time to and from the appearance paid at **1.5x** normal rate of pay.

## 6. Leave, Vacation & Holiday Bidding

- **Annual Vacation Entitlement ([Article 43, Sections 1–2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L538-L561); [Article 21, Section 6](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L242)):**
  - All vacation days equal 1 full shift day regardless of shift length (8, 8.5, or 10 hours).
  - **Schedule by Years of Service Completed During Calendar Year:**
    - **0 – 6 months (probationary):** None *(Lateral transfers receive 7 days upon hire + 5 days at 6 months)*
    - **6 months – 1 year:** 7 days
    - **1 – 3 years:** 12 days
    - **4 – 5 years:** 18 days
    - **6 years:** 19 days | **7 years:** 20 days | **8 years:** 21 days | **9 years:** 22 days | **10 years:** 23 days
    - **11 years:** 24 days | **12 years:** 25 days | **13 years:** 26 days | **14 years:** 27 days | **15 years:** 28 days
    - **16 years:** 29 days | **17 years:** 30 days | **18 years:** 31 days | **19 years:** 32 days | **20+ years:** 34 days
- **Vacation Bidding Rules ([Article 43, Sections 3–5](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L563-L570)):**
  - **January Preferential Bid ([Article 43, Section 3](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L563)):**
    - During January, employees may submit **2 individual requests** (Primary Bid and Secondary Bid) for consecutive vacation days: **4 to 15 days** for a 5-2 rotation, or **2 to 16 days** for a 4-2 rotation.
    - All **Primary Bids** are awarded first by seniority; after all Primary Bids are awarded, **Secondary Bids** are awarded by seniority.
    - If **both** Primary and Secondary bids are denied, the employee has **14 days** from the denial to submit **1 additional Primary Bid**, awarded subject to minimum count.
    - Approved Preferential Bids are honored regardless of transfer, cannot be changed/rescinded without written permission of the Chief or designee, and exempt the officer from Special Event inversing ([Article 27](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L350)).
  - **Rolling Seniority Window (45 to 15 Days Out) ([Article 43, Section 3(A)](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L564)):** After January, vacation requests are bid by **seniority** not more than **45 days** and not less than **15 days** prior to the requested date.
  - **Inside 15 Days (First-Come, First-Served) ([Article 43, Section 3(B)](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L565)):** Less than 15 days prior, vacations are granted on a **first-come, first-served** basis. Multi-day requests (2+ days) must be granted for the entire block requested.
  - **Same-Day / Hourly Use ([Article 43, Section 4](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L567)):** Vacation, personal days, or holidays may be used without advance notice if the supervisor is notified before shift report time and shift minimum count is maintained. Vacation days may be taken **1 hour at a time** subject to minimum count.
  - **Vacation Sell-Back ([Article 43, Section 5](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L569)):** Up to **5 vacation days** per year may be sold back by notifying the Chief in writing by **April 15** (paid the first pay period after January 15 of the following year).
- **Personal Days & Banked Time Usage ([Article 44, Section 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L573); [Article 39, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L499); [Article 48](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L606-L608); [Article 49](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L610-L611)):**
  - **Personal Days ([Article 44, Section 1](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L573)):** **10 personal days** per year, usable at the officer's discretion (including in **1-hour increments**) subject to minimum count.
  - **FLSA / Non-FLSA Time ([Article 39, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L499)):** May be taken **1 hour at a time** subject to shift minimum count.
  - **Carryover & 400-Hour Non-FLSA Paydown ([Article 48](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L606-L608)):** Unused vacation, holiday, personal, FLSA, and Non-FLSA time carries over year-to-year. Non-FLSA hours exceeding **400 hours** at year-end are paid down to 400 hours by **February 28** of the following year (at current rank, previous year's hourly rate).
  - **Non-FLSA Time Donations ([Article 49](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L610-L611)):** Officers may transfer or receive up to **160 hours** of Non-FLSA time per calendar year for donation to another employee (more requires Chief approval for hardship).
- **Holiday Scheduling & Bidding ([Article 36, Sections 1–8](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L439-L465); [Article 38, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L483)):**
  - **12 Designated Holidays ([Article 36, Sections 1–2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L439-L453)):** New Year's Day, MLK Day, Easter, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veteran's Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Eve. MLK Day and Memorial Day are observed on the national day of observance; all others on the actual calendar date.
  - **Holiday Window ([Article 36, Section 7](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L463)):** Runs from **0600 on the holiday until 0630 the day after** (shifts starting at 0600 the day after do not receive holiday pay for 0600–0630).
  - **Holiday Bidding Timeline ([Article 36, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L453)):**
    - Officers on regularly scheduled days off are not subject to holiday scheduling.
    - **45 to 15 Days Prior:** 30-day bidding window for officers assigned to the shift, by seniority.
    - **15 Days Prior:** Bids are awarded to on-shift bidders by seniority.
    - **15 to 10 Days Prior (Unfilled Spots):** Opened to all patrol officers via the hire-back process; awarded **10 days prior** by seniority with preference to Operations patrol officers subject to minimum count.
    - **10 to 5 Days Prior (Inversing & Exchanges):** Remaining unfilled spots are filled by inverse seniority. Inversed officers may exchange the holiday with an officer on a regular day off up until **5 days prior**, when the holiday schedule locks.
  - **Holiday Compensation & Exchanges ([Article 36, Sections 3–8](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L455-L465); [Article 38, Section 2](PBA_Contract_2026.md#L483)):**
    - Working a holiday earns regular salary + **double time (2.0x)** + the **holiday back** (additional hours worked outside the regular shift on a holiday are paid at 2.0x to the next quarter hour).
    - If a holiday falls on a regular day off, the employee receives another day off. Holidays must be taken as a whole day.
    - If an inversed officer exchanges a holiday with an off-duty officer, the officer working the exchange receives the holiday pay and 1 holiday day back placed in their Non-FLSA time bank.

# FOP Rules
*(Source: 2026 Fort Wayne Fraternal Order of Police Labor Council Contract — Ranks of Sergeant & Lieutenant)*

## 1. Shift Scheduling & Hours of Work

- **Uniform Divisions Standard Schedule ([Article 10, Section 1](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L101)):**
  - Repeating 6-day rotation consisting of **4 days on and 2 days off** (4-2 schedule) at consecutive **8.5-hour shifts**.
- **Investigative (and Administrative) Division Schedule ([Article 10, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L108); [Article 21, Section 4](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L266)):**
  - Repeating 7-day rotation consisting of **5 days on and 2 days off** (5-2 schedule) at consecutive **8.0-hour shifts**.
  - **Detective Sergeant Weekend Duty ([Article 21, Section 4](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L266)):** Detective sergeants on-call when no detective sergeant is on duty ("weekend duty") receive **2 hours paid time per day** at their base hourly rate.
- **Emergency Schedule Changes ([Article 10, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L103-L106)):**
  - The Chief may temporarily change normal working hours during an "emergency situation" (defined as a need for additional manpower above minimum counts that could not be reasonably foreseen and cannot be met without changing work hours, lasting only while the emergency exists).
- **In-Service Training Following C Shift ([Article 10, Section 5](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L115)):**
  - A member assigned to In-service training during A-shift hours immediately following a scheduled C shift is marked off-duty for the **last 4 hours** of the prior C shift and may mark off the first half of that shift (4.5 hours for Operations / 4 hours for Detective Bureau) using available banked time.
- **Temporary Assignments ([Article 10, Section 4](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L110-L113)):**
  - Maximum of **4 Sergeants or Lieutenants** on temporary assignment at any one time, for no more than **90 calendar days** within any 12-month period (except up to **160 days** at the Training Academy during a basic recruit class, or up to **180 days** in the SRO program).
  - No more than **1 Sergeant or Lieutenant** from a given Shift/Division may be placed on a temporary assignment at any time.
  - Appointees must have at least **3 months in rank** (except for Internal Affairs, Academy, Crime Scene, or Spillman Data Systems).
- **Exempt Assignments ([Article 9, Section 4(A)–(B)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L92-L96)):**
  - Up to **25% plus 1** of the bargaining unit (rounded up if fraction is ≥0.5%) may be assigned to exempt positions on a voluntary basis with 5 days' notice to the FOP.
  - Requires at least **3 months in rank** (except Internal Affairs, Academy, Crime Scene, or Spillman Data Systems).
  - Members accepting an exempt position have **7 calendar days** from the first day on the job to rescind and return to their previous position. Work hours for exempt employees are set by the Chief, subject to Article 28 overtime rules.
- **Commander of the Day (COD) Scheduling ([Article 25](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L339-L342)):**
  - In the absence of a Captain and/or Lieutenant, the **senior Sergeant** on a shift or division is placed in charge as COD and **cannot defer** the position.
  - COD receives **2 hours paid time** per shift per day (or **1 hour** if assigned as COD for 4 hours or less; if two Sergeants share COD duties, 1 hour per Sergeant, max 2 hours per shift per day).
- **Exchange of Work Time ([Article 19, Sections 1–3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L242-L246); [Article 20, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L252); [Article 16, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L218)):**
  - Members may exchange work time with members in the same like work (uniform with uniform, investigative with investigative) up to **160 hours** for a 5-2 rotation and **170 hours** for a 4-2 rotation (20 days of exchange time). Holidays are excluded from these limits.
  - Exchange forms must be signed by both members or submitted electronically and approved by the appropriate shift commander; once approved, it is a binding agreement.
  - **Exchanges Above 400 Non-FLSA Hours ([Article 20, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L252)):** If a member at the 400-hour Non-FLSA cap works for another member in exchange for donated Non-FLSA hours, those hours are recorded as "exchanged time" and must be used prior to the final paycheck of the year received (or the following year if donated after the final paycheck), or they are forfeited.
  - **Military Drill Exchanges ([Article 16, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L218)):** Members may exchange scheduled military drill days with regular days off and pay back the exchanged day(s) during the rotation immediately prior to and/or following the drill.

## 2. Seniority & Tie-Breakers

- **Seniority Definition & Tie-Breaker ([Article 9, Section 1](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L81)):**
  - Seniority is defined as an officer's most recent date of appointment by the Merit Commission to the rank of Sergeant or Lieutenant.
  - **Tie-Breaker:** If two or more officers have the same appointment date as a Sergeant or Lieutenant, seniority ranking is based on the **officer's position on the Merit Commission Promotion List**.
- **Vacancy Bidding & Transfer Rules ([Article 9, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L85-L89)):**
  - Vacancies due to retirement, discipline, new positions, or promotion to Captain or above must be bid within **15 calendar days**.
  - **How Bids Are Structured:**
    - **Operations Division (Sergeants):** Job-specific by Division, Section, Shift, and Rank, bid department-wide (e.g., Operations Division, Northwest Quadrant, B Shift, Sergeant).
    - **Investigative Support Division:** Bid by Division, Shift, and Rank. Prior to bidding, voluntary movement by seniority within the Investigative Support Division to the open position is allowed with management approval (movement between shifts is not permitted without a bid).
    - **Lieutenants:** Bid by Division, Shift, and Rank.
  - **Start Date & 10-Day Rescission Period ([Article 9, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L85-L87)):**
    - The start date must be within **21 days** of the award date.
    - Awarded bidders have **10 calendar days from the start date** to rescind and return to their previously held position (for members on FMLA leave, the start date and 10-day rescission window begin on their first workday back from FMLA). Rescinded bids go to the next senior bidder.
  - **Unbid / Displaced Supervisors & Inversing ([Article 9, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L88-L89)):**
    - A supervisor vacating or removed from an exempt assignment, demoted back to merit rank, or removed from a bid position due to discipline is temporarily assigned to a Uniform position on A, B, or C shift (determined by the Assistant Chief) until awarded a bid.
    - If no supervisor bids on a posted opening and there are no other unbid supervisors, the temporarily assigned supervisor is **inversed** into the open job (however, a supervisor removed from a bid position due to discipline may **not** be inversed back into that same position).
- **On-Call Extra Duty Assignment Tie-Breaker ([Article 9, Section 4(C)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L97)):**
  - Vacancies in on-call extra duty assignments are posted via department memo with qualifications and selection criteria. If two or more members pass and are ranked the same in the selection process, **seniority is the determining factor** (except for Public Information Officer [PIO], which the Chief may select regardless of seniority).

## 3. Overtime Rotation, Hire Backs & Special Events

- **FLSA Overtime & Additional Compensation ([Article 28, Section 1](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L357); [Article 21, Sections 2–3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L259-L264)):**
  - All FLSA overtime hours are paid at **1.5x** the employee's FLSA rate.
  - **Supervisory Training Overtime ([Article 21, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L259-L262)):** A Sergeant or Lieutenant training another Sergeant (minimum 15 working days) or Lieutenant (minimum 8 working days) receives **2.5 hours of paid overtime** per day of training.
  - **Off-Duty Car Maintenance ([Article 21, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L264)):** C-shift members (or others directed by command) required to bring their vehicle in for scheduled repair outside normal hours when the shop is closed during their shift are compensated under Article 28.
- **Hire-Back Procedures & Rotation ([Article 24, Sections 1–5](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L323-L335)):**
  - **Rate ([Article 24, Section 5](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L335)):** Paid at **1.5x** normal rate of pay.
  - **Notice & Award Order ([Article 24, Section 4](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L332)):**
    1. **≥ 24 Hours' Notice:** Posted department-wide and filled by **seniority**, with preference given to Uniform Division members (and primary assigned division members for quadrant minimums per [Section 2(B)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L327)).
    2. **< 24 Hours' Notice:** Announced via police radio channels, in-car messaging, and phone paging system; awarded on a **first-come, first-served** basis.
    3. **Subsequent Hire-Backs for the Same Shift:** Remaining supervisors who were denied the initial hire-back are contacted in list order (by **seniority** if >24 hours' notice; in **order received** if <24 hours' notice) via one phone call to their FLSA-listed number before re-posting.
  - **Partial-Shift & Post-Shift-Start Restrictions ([Article 24, Sections 3–4](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L330-L333)):**
    - A partial-shift hire-back is awarded **only** when a Lieutenant is absent for less than a full shift.
    - Once a shift has started, the COD position does not change unless vacated, and the hire-back procedure does not go into effect once the shift has started unless authorized by the Duty Chief.
    - **No Inversing for Regular Hire-Backs:** Inverse seniority is **never** used to fill a regular shift hire-back vacancy (used only for holiday bids).
- **Special Events Staffing & Overtime Rotation ([Article 22, Sections 1–2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L272-L292)):**
  - **Supervisory Ratios ([Article 22, Section 1](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L273)):**
    - Whenever **1 or more patrol officers** are bid to a Special Event, **1 Sergeant** must be bid (ratio may not exceed **6 patrol officers to 1 Sergeant**).
    - Whenever **4 Sergeants** are bid to a Special Event, **1 Lieutenant** must be bid (plus 1 additional Lieutenant for every additional 4 Sergeants).
    - Supervisors at Special Events are used in a supervisory capacity and not assigned patrol duties except in last-minute/emergency circumstances ([Section 2(O)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L292)).
  - **Order of Staffing & Bidding ([Article 22, Section 2(A)–(E), (K), (N)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L277-L291)):**
    1. Movement within the shift (without disrupting normal staffing).
    2. Volunteers working off-duty hours, scheduled days off, or non-illness leave days (bidding handled in the same manner as holiday bidding; Three Rivers Festival bid 45 days prior and awarded ≥15 days prior).
    3. **Department-Wide Inverse Seniority ([Section 2(C)–(E), (K)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L279-L288)):** If volunteers are insufficient, department-wide inverse seniority is used. Inversed members may have their work hours and/or days off changed for the duration of the event (and do not receive the 2.0x Special Event rate if shifted under Paragraph D, per [Section 2(H)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L285)).
       - **Exemptions from Special Event Inversing:** Members in the **Vice and Narcotics Bureau** and designated **Crime Scene technicians** ([Section 2(E)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L281)).
       - **Cross-Union Exchange:** Once inverse seniority is used, FOPLC members may exchange with any PBA member who was inversed to the Special Event ([Section 2(K)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L288)).
    4. **Celebrity/Dignitary Visits ([Section 2(N)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L291)):** If EST, PSRT, and EOD are absent or additional supervisors are needed, bid by seniority (or if time is insufficient, solicited via phone/messaging/email/dispatch, and if still unfilled, a less senior member may be selected). Paid at double time (2.0x).
  - **Special Event Compensation & Cancellation ([Article 22, Section 2(F)–(M)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L282-L290)):**
    - Paid at **double time (2.0x)**, calculated as `(base salary + shift differential + longevity pay + specialty duty bonuses) / 2080`.
    - Working a Special Event on a holiday earns holiday premium compensation plus the holiday "day back" if working **4 or more hours** (and not in an exchange for a PBA member).
    - Cancelled with **< 24 hours' notice:** Scheduled members receive **2 hours' pay** at the Special Event rate. If cancelled after reporting (or relieved without fault), the member contacts the COD for reassignment or is paid for the balance of the event.
- **Sick Leave / Light Duty Bidding Restriction ([Article 26](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L348)):**
  - Members on sick leave or light duty may bid to work a holiday, Special Event, or hire-back, but if they are not released to full duty by the award date (**15 days prior**), their bid is voided and awarded to the next senior bidder.

## 4. Minimum Staffing

- **Uniform Division & Quadrant Minimum Counts ([Article 24, Section 2(A)–(B)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L325-L327)):**
  - **Shift Minimum Count:** **4 Sergeants** in the Uniform Division, plus **1 Lieutenant** (5 supervisors total).
  - **Quadrant Minimum Count:** **1 Uniform Division Sergeant per quadrant** (if a quadrant falls below 1 Sergeant, a hire-back is initiated with preference to primary assigned division members).
  - **Absence of a Lieutenant / COD Hire-Back Rule ([Article 24, Section 2(A)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L326)):**
    - If no Lieutenant is working, a hire-back is initiated and awarded to the senior Lieutenant.
    - If no Lieutenant takes the hire-back and at least **5 Sergeants** are working, the senior Sergeant becomes COD.
    - If no Lieutenant is working and only **4 Sergeants** are working (covering the 4 quadrants), a Sergeant hire-back is initiated to cover the COD position (awarded to the senior bidding Sergeant, with Uniform Division preference, while the most senior Sergeant working the shift serves as COD).
- **Holiday Minimum Counts ([Article 13, Sections 2–3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L192-L198); [Article 24, Section 2(C)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L328)):**
  - The City reserves the right to set minimum counts by holiday and by rank ([Article 13, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L198)).
  - Standard Operations Division holiday work level is **1 Lieutenant and 4 Sergeants (5 supervisors)**.
- **Exclusions & Exemptions from Minimum Count:**
  - **Newly Promoted Sergeants in Training ([Article 21, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L261)):** During their 15-day training period, newly promoted Sergeants do not affect the minimum count of the assigned division.
  - **Extended Bereavement Leave ([Article 17(D)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L233)):** Up to **5 days** from any available time bank for extended family funerals or extended bereavement leave must be granted **not subject to minimum counts**.
  - **FOP Time Bank ([Article 7, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L45-L46)):** Use of FOP Labor Council time bank hours is subject to maintaining minimum manpower requirements.

## 5. Callback / Call-Out & Court Pay

- **Call-Out Definition & Holiday Window ([Article 23, Section 1(A)–(B)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L297-L298)):**
  - **Call-Out:** Required immediate response to an emergency situation, warrant service, protective detail, or other situation deemed necessary by the Chief or designee with **48 hours' notice or less**.
  - **Holiday Window (for Call-Outs):** Begins at **0500 hours** on the actual day of the holiday and continues until **0600 hours** the day following the holiday.
- **Call-Out Bonus Pay ([Article 23, Sections 2–3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L300-L303)):**
  - **Begins AND Ends Outside Regular Shift:** Bonus of **2 paid hours**.
  - **Either Begins OR Ends Outside Regular Shift:** Bonus of **1 paid hour**.
  - **Holiday Call-Out Pay ([Article 23, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L302-L303)):**
    - Hours worked during the holiday window (0500 to 0600 next day) are paid at **double time (2.0x)**.
    - If the call-out continues past 0600 into the day after the holiday, hours worked on the following day (outside normal shift hours) are paid at **time and one-half (1.5x)**.
    - If a holiday call-out **exceeds 4 hours**, the member also receives the **holiday back**.
- **Mandatory Response On-Call Compensation ([Article 23, Section 4(A)–(F)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L305-L319)):**
  - **Weekly On-Call Supervisors ([Section 4(A)–(C)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L306-L308)):**
    - **Homicide Team Supervisor, Crime Scene Supervisor, or Fatal Crash Investigation Supervisor:** **1 full day** (8 or 8.5 hours) credited to their On-Call time bank for each completed primary on-call week.
    - **Air Support Unit (ASU):** **1/2 day** for the primary week and **1/2 day** for the back-up week.
    - **Fatal Crash Team Member (non-supervisor):** **1/2 day** for the primary week.
  - **Annual Specialty Team On-Call Time Bank ([Section 4(D)–(F)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L309-L319)):** Credited at the start of each year (at 8 or 8.5 hours per day depending on shift):
    - **EST:** 4 days
    - **CRT:** 4 days
    - **ASU (if Section 4B is not applicable):** 4 days
    - **PIO:** 2 days
    - **EOD:** 2 days
    - **EOD K9:** 2 days
    - **Shooting / Use of Force Team:** 2 days
    - **PSRT:** 1 day per call-out lasting >4 hours (max 1 day back per 24 hours of a call-out).
    - *Attendance & Forfeiture Rule:* Requires ≥60% call-out attendance rate (excluding prolonged illness/injury) to retain the following year's allotment. All on-call hours earned in a calendar year must be used prior to the subsequent year's Non-FLSA paydown or they are forfeited.
- **Off-Duty Internal Affairs / OPS Interviews ([Article 11, Section 5(D)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L134)):**
  - Supervisors questioned or interviewed off-duty are compensated at **1.5x (time and a half)** for the time spent in the interview **plus 1 hour of travel time**.
- **Off-Duty Court Appearance Pay ([Article 21, Section 1(A)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L256-L257)):**
  - Paid at **double time (2.0x)** with a **1-hour minimum** per appearance (not per subpoena), rounded up to the nearest quarter hour beyond the first hour.

## 6. Leave, Vacation & Holiday Bidding

- **Annual Vacation Entitlement ([Article 12, Sections 1–2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L160-L180)):**
  - All vacation days equal 1 full shift day regardless of shift length.
  - **Schedule by Years of Service Completed During Calendar Year:**
    - **3 – 5 years:** 18 days
    - **6 years:** 19 days | **7 years:** 20 days | **8 years:** 21 days | **9 years:** 22 days | **10 years:** 23 days
    - **11 years:** 24 days | **12 years:** 25 days | **13 years:** 26 days | **14 years:** 27 days | **15 years:** 28 days
    - **16 years:** 29 days | **17 years:** 31 days | **18 years:** 32 days | **19 years:** 33 days | **20+ years:** 34 days
- **Vacation Bidding Rules ([Article 12, Sections 3–4](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L182-L185)):**
  - **January & June Preferential Bids ([Article 12, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L182)):**
    - **January:** Members may request **2 separate vacation periods** of **4 to 16 consecutive vacation days** each.
    - **June:** Members may request **1 separate vacation period** of **4 to 16 consecutive vacation days**.
    - Competing requests for the same consecutive days are awarded to the **most senior employee**. Once approved, requests are honored regardless of transfers and **may not be changed or rescinded**.
  - **Rolling Seniority Window (45 to 15 Days Out) ([Article 12, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L183)):** Subsequent to January, annual vacation days are bid by **seniority** not more than **45 days** and not less than **15 days** prior to the requested date.
  - **Same-Day / Hourly Use ([Article 12, Section 4](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L185)):** Vacation, personal days, or holidays may be used without advance notice if the supervisor is notified before shift report time and shift minimum count is maintained. Vacation and personal days may be taken **1 hour at a time** subject to minimum count.
- **Personal Days & Non-FLSA Time Bank Rules ([Article 18](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L238); [Article 20, Sections 1–2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L250-L252); [Article 28, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L359)):**
  - **Personal Days ([Article 18](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L238)):** **12 personal days** per year, usable in **1-hour increments** subject to minimum staffing.
  - **400-Hour Non-FLSA Cap & Paydown ([Article 28, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L359)):** Non-FLSA hours exceeding **400 hours** at year-end (including carryover) are paid down to 400 hours on or before **January 31** of the following year (at current rank, previous year's hourly rate; hours must be submitted to the Chief's Office by **January 15**).
  - **Non-FLSA Time Donations ([Article 20, Section 1](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L250)):** Members may transfer or receive up to **160 hours** of Non-FLSA time per calendar year for donation to another member (cannot exceed the recipient's 400-hour Non-FLSA limit, though members above 400 hours can still earn Non-FLSA hours via holiday Special Events or call-outs; hardship transfers >160 hours require Chief approval).
- **Holiday Scheduling & Bidding ([Article 13, Sections 1–3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L189-L198); [Article 19, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L244); [Article 24, Section 2(C)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L328)):**
  - **12 Designated Holidays ([Article 13, Sections 1–2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L189-L192)):** New Year's Day, MLK's Birthday, Easter, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veteran's Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Eve. **All holidays are observed on the actual calendar date of the holiday.**
  - **Holiday Bidding & Inversing Procedure ([Article 13, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L192-L194); [Article 24, Section 2(C)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L328)):**
    - Members on regularly scheduled days off are not subject to holiday scheduling.
    - **45 to 15 Days Prior:** 30-day bidding window by seniority.
    - **Order of Award (Uniform Division):**
      1. Awarded by seniority to on-shift Uniform Division members.
      2. If spots remain unfilled on a shift, Uniform Division members on **any shift** may bid, awarded by seniority.
      3. If still insufficient to meet minimum manning (4 Sergeants), **inverse seniority** (excluding officers regularly scheduled off) is used to fill the Sergeant vacancy.
      4. If no Lieutenant is working the holiday, the most senior Sergeant is awarded the holiday COD bid (compensated at the holiday rate plus COD pay per Article 25).
    - **Posting Deadline:** The completed holiday schedule must be posted at least **15 days prior** to the holiday.
    - **Post-Award Absence Replacement ([Article 24, Section 2(C)](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L328)):** If an awarded supervisor cannot work the holiday due to illness, funeral leave, childbirth, etc., the COD must exhaust the list of denied on-shift Sergeants and Lieutenants in order of seniority before issuing a hire-back request.
  - **Holiday Compensation & Exchanges ([Article 13, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L196-L197); [Article 19, Section 2](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L244)):**
    - Working a holiday earns regular salary + **paid double time (2.0x)** + the **holiday back**.
    - **Overtime on a Holiday ([Article 13, Section 3](FOPLC_Contract_2026.md#L196)):** Additional hours worked on a holiday **outside** the member's regularly scheduled shift are paid at **double time and one-half (2.5x)** for each additional **0.5 hour** worked.
    - If a holiday falls on a regular day off, military leave, or Hurt on Duty status, the member retains the holiday.
    - Any member inversed on a holiday may exchange with a member on a regular day off; the member working the exchange receives the holiday pay and 1 holiday day back in their Non-FLSA time bank.
